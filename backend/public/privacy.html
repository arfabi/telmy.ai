Data Transparency & Security

Telmy.ai Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Core Privacy Commitment (Privacy-First)

Telmy.ai ("we", "our", or "the Service") is strictly committed to protecting the privacy of our users. Our core operational design is Privacy-by-Architecture: Telmy.ai only inspects public form schema metadata so the AI assistant can provide accurate, contextual field guidance. We never collect, sell, or monetize your personal information.

2. Information We Process

When you activate Telmy.ai on a web page, the extension only parses structural interface metadata of the form, which includes:

  • Form labels and question titles (e.g., "Place of Birth", "Passport Number").
  • Input element types (text, date picker, multiple-choice, select dropdown, checkbox).
  • Default placeholder text and validation patterns (expected date format, numerical digits).

3. Information We NEVER Collect or Store

Telmy.ai NEVER reads, captures, transmits, or stores user-inputted sensitive values entered into form fields, including but not limited to:

  • National Identification Numbers (NIK/SSN), passport numbers, or official tax IDs.
  • Passwords, PINs, or authentication security codes.
  • Bank account numbers, credit card credentials, or financial payment details.
  • Personal uploaded documents, certificates, or third-party file attachments.

4. Push-to-Talk Microphone Privacy

Telmy.ai features an on-demand voice assistant designed strictly around the Push-to-Talk principle. The microphone is only enabled when you explicitly click the microphone button in the native side panel, and automatically shuts off when you finish speaking. We never use wake-words or continuous background listening. Audio stream data is only utilized for live speech-to-text question transcription.

5. Secure Cloud AI Processing

Contextual reasoning and voice synthesis requests are processed via secure, encrypted cloud APIs. Field metadata transmitted for guidance contains zero personally identifiable information (PII).

6. Chrome Web Store Policy Compliance

The Telmy.ai extension adheres strictly to the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies and the Limited Use requirements. Form metadata is never transferred to third-party data brokers, used for advertising targeting, or used for credit eligibility determinations.

7. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact our team via: privacy@telmy.id or support@telmyai.com.
