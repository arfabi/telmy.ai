Built For Trust

Your personal information stays yours

Passports and ID numbers are private. We built Telmy to protect them.

🛡️
Zero-Knowledge Client-Side Encryption (AES-256) Your profile data is locked on your device using bank-grade AES-256 with keys derived from your 6-Digit Master PIN. Data is encrypted before ever leaving your browser.
🗝️
Admins & Developers Cannot Peek Our cloud servers and database administrators only see scrambled ciphertext. Without your Master PIN, reading your profile data is mathematically impossible.
🗑️
Zero Physical Document Retention Physical ID cards and academic diplomas processed for entity extraction are permanently shredded from server memory immediately after scanning—zero file storage on disk or cloud.
🚫
Strict Security Exclusions & Never Sold Passwords, CVVs, credit cards, and OTPs are strictly skipped by our AutoFill engine. We never sell or monetize your personal information. Ever.
